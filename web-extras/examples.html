④ Paper playground — play with recent research

Prime Attention: per-pair modulated attention for MTS arXiv 2509.12196 · ~5–15 min

Scenario: General Model Implementation. A simplified, CPU-runnable reproduction of Dynamic Relational Priming Improves Transformer in Multivariate Time Series (Lee & Clark): keys/values are modulated by a pair-specific “primer” built from FFT lead-lag cross-correlations, then compared head-to-head with standard attention on synthetic data with heterogeneous channel relationships.

Input: bundled spec Prime Attention mini (view PDF). PyTorch (CPU) model built from scratch by the agent, seeds fixed, runtime < 10 min. Watch the dashboard to see the agent implement, run and iterate on it.

ChaTSFM: channel adapter for frozen TS foundation models ICML 2026 · ~5–15 min

Scenario: General Model Implementation. A simplified reproduction of Channel Adapter for Time Series Foundation Models in Zero-Shot Multivariate Forecasting (Li et al.): a frozen channel-independent backbone plus a tiny (8-parameter) adapter — descriptor-conditioned channel similarity + gated sparse blending — pre-trained on a budgeted synthetic corpus and evaluated zero-shot.

Input: bundled spec ChaTSFM mini (view PDF). PyTorch (CPU) model built from scratch by the agent, seeds fixed, runtime < 10 min.

⑤ Architecture lab — build & test a custom quant architecture

Stock-ranking Mixture-of-Experts on real market data 16 real stocks · ~5–15 min

Scenario: General Model Implementation. Implement the MoE ranking architecture — feature encoder → temporal mixer → three experts (industry/sector, dynamic-KNN, global latent-factor tokens) combined by a regime router g₁,g₂,g₃ → cross-asset ranking/return forecast — then evaluate it on the bundled dataset: one year of real public daily closes for 16 US large-caps across 4 GICS-style sectors.

Input: bundled spec Stock-MoE spec + embedded dataset (view PDF). PyTorch (CPU) model built from scratch by the agent, seed fixed, runtime < 15 min. Reference result: routing turns individually-unprofitable experts into positive rank IC and long-short returns.
